About ePlanet Speakers Academy

English Becomes Easy When You Understand the Concept

At ePlanet Speakers Academy, we believe English becomes easy when you understand the concept. Our teaching method focuses on practical learning instead of making students simply copy notes and memorize things. We help students understand English in an easy way and gradually start speaking it in real-life situations.

In many traditional classrooms, hours are spent writing notes in notebooks and memorizing grammar rules. Yet when it comes to speaking with friends, in interviews, or in public, hesitation takes over. At ePlanet, we change that by putting understanding and speaking first.
